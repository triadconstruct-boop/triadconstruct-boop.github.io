# Early-warning rebuild audit — 2026-09-09

## Pre-rebuild findings

The v2 engine treated each article as an event, used ten mostly primary sources, measured corroboration by outlet name, collapsed authority/impact into severity, and exposed only one machine score for each strategic product. It did not retain a claim lifecycle, contradictions, refutations, dormancy, historical patterns, confidence components, or a reliable distinction between a quiet world and impaired collection.

False-negative risks included narrow source breadth, primary-source publication lag, exact keyword dependence, first-region-only geography, shallow actor detection, 48-hour title similarity, topic-word activation of strategic vectors, aggressive freshness limits, and no path for high-impact speculation to reach WORLDWATCH.

False-positive controls existed, but some were blunt: hard thresholds could erase useful signals rather than preserve and label them. Repeated wire stories could appear independent because attribution roots were not traced.

## Remediation

- Expanded to 61 catalog entries / 60 enabled public sources across ten classes.
- Split observation, provenance, claim, audit, memory, anomaly, confidence, severity, threat, and watch records.
- Added seven explicit claim states and deterministic lifecycle gates.
- Added nine-component confidence with contradiction and material-time decay.
- Added multi-domain, multi-region, and actor tagging; cross-domain linkage and convergence anomalies.
- Added branch hypotheses, confirmation indicators, and falsifiers.
- Added separated WWT and THRESHOLD hard/weak channels.
- Added source/ingestion/coverage/state/confidence/update diagnostics and explicit quiet-state interpretation.
- Added 13 regression tests, including historical cases and false-negative controls.

## Remaining known limitations

Rules and title/text clustering are deterministic and cannot resolve every alias, translation, location, ownership chain, or evolving event boundary. Source reliability priors require periodic human review. Public feeds may disappear without notice. Current anomalies indicate correlation, not causation. Historical recurrence raises watch priority by a bounded amount and never raises confidence or changes state by itself.

These limitations are surfaced, not concealed; the architecture supports future entity resolution, licensed adapters, reviewed overrides, language-specific rules, and learned calibration without changing the evidence-state contract.
