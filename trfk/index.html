WHAT TRFK TRACKS

  • Organizations named in court records or official reporting.
  • Case clusters documented by INTERPOL, Europol, UNODC or national law enforcement.
  • Routes, industries and infrastructure only at non-tactical public-source resolution.
  • Relationships with a source and an explicit evidence state.

WHAT TRFK EXCLUDES

  • Victim identities, testimony details that increase exposure, or private addresses.
  • Unsupported social-media allegations and anonymous name lists.
  • Claims that shared geography or payment methods prove coordination.
  • Migrant-smuggling cases unless exploitation or a trafficking nexus is documented.

GRAPH INTERPRETATION

A connection to a shared hub means common exposure or infrastructure—not partnership. Solid red edges represent an officially documented relationship. Dashed edges represent analytical overlap and must not be treated as proof of coordination.
